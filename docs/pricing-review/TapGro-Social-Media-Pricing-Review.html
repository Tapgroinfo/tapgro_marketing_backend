1. Competitor review

Grexa — grexa.ai

“AI marketing team” for local businesses (clinics, salons, gyms). Claims 500,000+ businesses; official Meta Business Partner and Google Partner. Managed from the Grexa mobile app.

  • GBP AI Agent: SEO profile rewrite, asks for reviews, replies to all reviews, daily posts, photo scheduler, performance analytics.
  • WhatsApp Chat Agent: answers enquiries, suggests slots, confirms bookings, follows up, multilingual.
  • WhatsApp Marketing Agent: occasion campaigns, brochures, reminders.
  • Not offered: Facebook / Instagram posting, YouTube, Meta ads.

4. Option B — Zernio (one API for all platforms)

Zernio charges per connected account per month. One Facebook Page, one Instagram, one Google Business Profile, one YouTube channel or one Meta Ads account = 1 account each. All features are included (posting, analytics, comments, reviews, webhooks). Uses Zernio's own approved apps, so our Meta suspension does not block it.

6. Assumptions & what is not included

  • Exchange rate ₹88 per $1. Zernio bills a card in USD; add about 3–4% for card forex charges.
  • Not included in API cost: our servers and video storage (S3), AI content generation (captions, review-reply text, Reels), support staff, payment gateway fees, GST.
  • Developer cost for Option A is an assumption (₹1,00,000 per developer per month) — replace with real numbers.
  • Grexa USD prices are from a third-party review read 28 Oct 2026; India prices from grexa.ai/pricing on 4 Nov 2026.
  • Zernio could change its pricing; it publishes a full itemised invoice each month.
